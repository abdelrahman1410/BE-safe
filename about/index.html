I'm a senior HSE professional with 10 years of progressive experience with some of the largest companies in Saudi Arabia and Egypt, from junior HSE officer to deputy HSE manager on a mega MEP project serving the Haram expansion in Makkah, and leading an HSE technical office on a national housing project.

My work covers HSE governance, risk assessment, permit-to-work, incident investigation and training, with a strong focus on reporting and performance measurement. I also build digital HSE tools in Power BI, AppSheet and SharePoint to make safety data clear and useful.

I created BE SAFE to share practical tools, templates and know-how with HSE teams and site engineers.
